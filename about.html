About us

Built on trust, designed for life.

PNR Architecture is an Ameerpet-based design studio working across architecture, interiors, and structural engineering — for clients building across India, on-site or fully online.

Our studio

One team, from first sketch to final structural detail

PNR Architecture was built around a simple problem: architectural design and structural engineering are usually handled by separate people who don't talk to each other enough, and the client absorbs the gap between them.

We keep design and structural work in the same studio — the same team that draws your floor plan also details the column and footing sizes that hold it up, and works out the steel structure if your project needs one. That means fewer surprises on site, and a single point of contact for the whole drawing set: architectural, interior, civil, and steel.

We work with residential and commercial clients directly, and take on freelance and consultancy work for other studios and contractors — on-site in Hyderabad or fully online, anywhere in India.
